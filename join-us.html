01 Details

General Requirements

Before registering for Membership or applying for any Employee position, make sure you meet every requirement below.

  • You must be at least 13 years of age to register for membership or hold a position in Placeholder Systems.
  • You must have read and agreed to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service in full before submitting either form on this page. By registering or submitting the application form, you affirm that you have read and accepted both documents.
  • You may hold only one Placeholder Account™ and, separately, only one active Employee record . Duplicate or alt accounts discovered during review will be merged or terminated.
  • Your application(s) are typically reviewed within seven business days. You'll be contacted at the email address you provided.

Departments

Every standard position sits within one of the four departments below. Pick the one that matches your skills and interests when filling out the application. You may change departments if you later think that it was unsuitable for you.

Marketing Department
Responsible for creating and executing promotional strategies and managing our social media platforms.
  • seeking social media manager who will also be the marketing director
Security Department
Ensures the security of our operations and data. Covers account security reviews, monitoring for abuse of our projects or copyright violations.
  • no open positions
Administrative Department
Manages human resource, financial and legal matters.
  • due to the sensitive nature of this dept, application without a direct reccommendation is strongly discouraged.
Research & Development Department
Develops, maintains and updates projects and internal systems.
  • looking for people with experience in google apps script, css/html/js and proxy networks
Executive Board (not really a department)
People in charge of running Placeholder Systems make up this board, including Chief Regional Executives, Directors and Managers (deputy directors).
  • application for executive roles is restricted to employees only

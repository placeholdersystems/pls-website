FAQ

Questions, answered.

Why was Placeholder Systems founded? +
Originally, we were founded to manage "methods" for bypassing internet filtering agents. Though now we no longer partake in such activities, we still love games and stuff, so we wanted to make some more accessable for everyone. (and maybe make some money)
What is OPSTAT? +
Our live operational-status indicator, left over from more security oriented times. We now use it to display response times and closures because it looked cool.
How do I apply to join the team? +
We are looking for people to help with marketing and development,if you are interested head to the Join Us page.
Is this a company, or a side project? +
Both, though we are not a registered company, we still hold our work to such standards while still doing this in our free time.
What are Placeholder Accounts, and how do I create one? +
A Placeholder Account is basically a membership, and you can use that account to login and get access to more features from our projects. Create one by heading to the Join Us page.
What was the rationale behind our brand colours and logo?+
Orange was one of the 3 main colours used since our inception, and the neon pink was inspired by our name, as placeholders, especially in games, use neon pink with black in a checkered pattern, which also led to the shape of our current logo.
How many people are involved in this? +
Across all branches, we have about 10 individuals supporting Placeholder Systems. How about you make that number increase and join us?

Privacy Policy

Effective: 07 January 2026
Last Revised: 25 August 2026
Jurisdiction: Singapore
01Introduction & Accountability
1.1

Placeholder Systems ("we", "us" or "The Company") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. In compliance with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA), we have appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO) to ensure our data practices remain transparent, secure, and legally compliant. Please note that the Security Director will always serve as the DPO, unless otherwise stated.

1.2

As used in this Policy:

  • "user" means any individual who uses or accesses the Services
  • "member" means an individual who has entered into a membership agreement with us
  • "interested party" means an individual/entity who has contacted us or may enter into a contract with us for the supply of goods or services
  • "employee" means an individual who has entered into a formal employment contract with us, including those providing services on a probationary, intern, or project-based basis
  • "personal data" means data, whether true or not, about a member, interested party, or employee who can be identified from that data or from that data and other information to which we have or are likely to have access
1.3

Other terms used in this Policy shall have the meanings given to them in the PDPA, where the context so permits.

02The Consent & Notification Obligation
2.1

We generally do not collect your personal data unless it is provided to us voluntarily by you directly after you have been notified of the purposes for which the data is collected and you have provided explicit consent, or collection and use of personal data without consent is permitted or required by the PDPA or other laws.

2.2

The Services are intended for users aged 13 and above. Users under the age of 13 must not access or use the Services. Certain content available through the Services may be subject to higher age thresholds; applicable age requirements are stated on the respective pages or content.

2.3

The consent that you provide for the collection, use and disclosure of your personal data will remain valid until such time it is being withdrawn by you in writing. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our DPO. Please note that depending on the nature and scope of your request, withdrawal of consent may result in termination of your membership, employment, or access to restricted technical assets.

03Personal Data We Collect
3.1

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate our Services. Depending on the nature of your interaction with us, some examples of personal data which we may collect from you include:

  • Email addresses
  • Records, and contract-related communications (Employment only)
  • Any information voluntarily sent through our forms
3.2

We and our hosting platform (Google Sites) may collect technical and usage data automatically when you access our Services, including IP addresses, browser types, and session duration through essential cookies or similar web tracking technologies required for security and site functionality.

04Purpose of Data Collection
4.1

We may collect and use your personal data for any or all of the following purposes:

  • Confirming identity and eligibility for membership or employment
  • Managing the employment relationship, including role assignments and internal communications
  • Maintaining the integrity of our systems and preventing unauthorized redistribution of assets
  • Sending security reminders, technical updates, news, or responding to direct inquiries
  • Maintaining records in accordance with our internal governance and compliance policies
  • Responding to, handling, and processing queries, requests, applications, complaints, and feedback from you
05Storage, Security, & Third-Party Services
5.1

This website is hosted on Google Sites, and data is collected through Google Apps Script and compiled in Google Sheets. Your data is subject to Google's security protocols in addition to our internal access controls.

5.2

We implement "Reasonable Security Arrangements" to safeguard your personal data from unauthorised access, collection, use, or disclosure. This includes administrative and technical measures such as two-factor authentication (2FA) for administrators and restricted access to data-storage sheets.

5.3

We do not sell, trade, or rent your data. We disclose information only to authorized internal executive members on a "need-to-know" basis. Information will never be disclosed to any third party except where strictly mandated by a lawful order.

5.4

The Services contain third-party content. We make no representations as to the quality, suitability, functionality or legality of such material. We do not monitor or investigate external third-party content providers and accept no responsibility or liability for any loss arising from their content. This Policy does not extend to your use of such external third-party content or websites. You are advised to read the privacy policy of such providers before using them.

5.5

As our operations utilize cloud infrastructure provided by Google, your personal data may be processed or transferred outside of Singapore. In such cases, we ensure that the transferred data receives a standard of protection comparable to the protection under the PDPA.

06Retention & Data Breach Notification
6.1

We retain your data only as long as your membership or employment contract is active or as required for operational security, or as required or permitted by applicable laws. Data that is no longer necessary for legal or business purposes will be permanently purged.

6.2

Breach Notification: In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in significant harm, we will notify affected Users, Members, and Employees within 1 calendar day.

07Your Rights
7.1

You have the right to make:

  • An access request for a copy of the personal data which we hold about you or information about the ways in which we use or disclose your personal data
  • A correction request to correct or update any of your personal data which we hold about you
7.2

We will respond to all such requests as soon as reasonably possible, generally within 5 business days or within the response time as stated on our operational status widget thingy.

08Contact Our Data Protection Officer
8.1

You may contact our Data Protection Officer if you have any enquiries or feedback on our personal data protection policies and procedures, or if you wish to make any request, at placeholdersystems@gmail.com using the following format:

Subject: [Data Protection; Requesting DPO] Body: Explain your enquiry/request clearly with supporting evidence if necessary.
8.2

If you are unsatisfied with our response to a data protection query or request, you maintain the right to submit a complaint or enquiry directly to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) of Singapore.

This section applies solely to residents of the United States and users accessing our services from US jurisdictions.

III. State Privacy Rights
  • You have the right to request a disclosure of the specific pieces of personal data we have collected about you over the past 12 months.
  • You have the right to request that we delete any personal data we have collected from you, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Placeholder Systems does not "sell" or "share" personal data (as those terms are defined under US state laws) to third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration.
  • We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.
  • Residents of the state of California may request a list of the categories of personal data (if any) we have disclosed to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. We currently do not disclose any personal data to third parties for marketing or any other purpose.
